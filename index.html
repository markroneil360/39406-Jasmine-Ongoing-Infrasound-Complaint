Evidence & interpretation

Only decoded AM.R19BB.00.HDF and AM.R19BB.00.EHZ records are accepted. The refresh checks identity, sample timing, overlap consistency and complete-minute coverage before computing statistics.

Raw counts are not calibrated pascals, sound-pressure levels or evidence of source, intent, harmful exposure or an ordinance violation. Sensor connectivity does not establish network security.

Analysis method & freshness rules

100 Hz sampling; 6,000 valid samples per UTC minute. Demean, apply a Hann window, then compute a full-minute FFT. HDF classification selects the highest-power bin from 1 through 20 Hz and tests whether it falls in 4 through 8 Hz inclusive. Gaps break consecutive runs. No interpolation or missing-time scoring.

Requests end 30 minutes before the refresh to allow archive lag. A sample less than 90 minutes old can establish recent acquisition at check time, not a continuously live connection. Snapshots older than 26 hours are marked stale. Seven-day coverage starts from the requested seven-day window, including uncollected time.

Previous historical page totals remain in Git history but are not imported into this new archive without their underlying evidence. Detroit measurements and private uploads are excluded.
