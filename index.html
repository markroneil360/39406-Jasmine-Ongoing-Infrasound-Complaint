REPRODUCIBLE BY DESIGN

Detroit rules, Northville data

Only AM.R19BB.00.HDF and AM.R19BB.00.EHZ at 100 Hz are accepted. Each complete UTC clock minute requires 6,000 finite, uncontested samples. The raw sample values are unchanged; fractional timestamps are assigned to the nearest 100 Hz tick within 5 milliseconds. No interpolation, resampling or zero filling.

Welch PSD uses 8-second Hann segments, 50% overlap, constant detrending and density scaling. The dominant band has the highest mean PSD among 1–4, 4–8, 8–16 and 16–20 Hz. Upper boundaries are excluded except 20 Hz. The primary sustained event is at least 10 consecutive complete 4–8 Hz-dominant minutes. Missing, partial, flatline or conflicting minutes break runs.
